My Store Hub

A ready medicine-store website you can sell to pharmacies and surgical shops. Try the catalogue, then send feedback.

Sign in

Shop and checkout

  • Public catalogue with stock and pack images
  • Cart, bill, and Checkout QR payment
  • My Orders with pending, approved, dispatched, rejected
  • Retail or wholesale prices after the store assigns a group

Store administration

  • Dashboard for today’s orders, revenue, and low stock
  • Upload and edit medicines with retail/wholesale price
  • Approve bills, generate PO, then dispatch
  • Add clients, block/allow login, notes, and CSV export
  • Employee add, view, update, remove
  • Sales reports and email report

Super Admin platform

  • Create isolated customer businesses (tenants)
  • Per-customer title, slogan, logo, QR, and theme
  • Login Initial such as {{ '@shubhsurgical' }} to load that store’s look
  • Plans, modules, backups, and website hit count
